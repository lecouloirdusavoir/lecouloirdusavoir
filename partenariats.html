🌐 For international partners — English summary

I am a Senior Education Inspector with over 15 years as an Inspector and more than 32 years of experience in the Senegalese national education system, serving the Ministry of National Education of Senegal. I have developed three original pedagogical frameworks: Stivation (2010), CREAQ (2015), and TDI (2019), applied across multiple inspection zones in West Africa. I have contributed to YouScribe since 2013. I am available for international conferences, benchmarking partnerships, scientific colloquia, and collaborative publications in Francophone and comparative education. I welcome contact from researchers, institutions, and NGOs working on teacher training, educational quality, and school leadership.
